# Workshop Notes

## The build queue

The order matters. The Singee language comes before the accent, because the accent borrows from it. The history comes before the university and the politics, because both stand on it. The two cities come last, because they draw on everything else.

1. **The repository and its house rules.** Done, November 2026.
2. **The road atlas.** Done, November 2026. *Road Atlas* v1.0, with the Master Reference updated to v3.0.
3. **Singee: sound and spelling.** Next. Adopted in conversation: four vowels (a, e, i, u) and no O, ever. Words end in a vowel or N. Stress falls on the root, so Isalune is ee-sah-LOO-neh, while the rest of the region says EYE-sah-loon. Three sounds the 1977 Authority clerks smoothed away: a catch in the throat, written with an apostrophe (Masiru'a); a breathy L, written lh (Lhunsalu, Isalhune); and a rasp at the back of the mouth, written x. The Nation's spelling uses keyboard letters only. The Nation writes the people's name Singi, said SING-ee. Nurven keeps its English spelling everywhere, by a 1973 council vote.
4. **Singee: grammar.** Verbs that mark how the speaker knows something (seen, heard, told), two words for "we" (including or leaving out the listener), and the other features that give the language its character.
5. **Singee: the word list.** Every word gets its Authority spelling and its Nation spelling, plus a short passage at the end.
6. **Singee history.** The 1890s ethnographer with his wax cylinders, who filed Singee as another people's dialect. The Nurven confederation, with Five Fords as its meeting ground. The fever years of the 1830s. Children sent to Chemawa under other nations' names. The 1969 testimony given in Singee. The 1973 spelling vote. The revival today, with a handful of elders and an immersion preschool at the Fold.
7. **The regional accent.** A local pronunciation for each place. The habit of putting "the" before everything. Chinook Jargon words plus a few Singee words everybody uses wrong. Milepost directions and the other idioms.
8. **The missing middle of the century.** The timber boom. The war years, with the Neptune shipyard, the Black neighborhood it made, and the Coast Guard patrols on the headlands. The 1990s timber wars and their Neptune twist.
9. **Legends and atmosphere.** The legend list, with each legend marked as told everywhere, true nowhere, or true somewhere and saved for the book that wants it. A ghost of Ichabod's Eye's own. A season-by-season record of the forest's smells and sounds.
10. **The University of Greater Neptune.** The flagship in the city of Neptune, UGNC in Cadence, Amity Gate, the old teachers' college in the Wilds, and the field station at Lake Varuna. The Nation's college at the Fold stays outside the system.
11. **Politics.** The real counties underneath the region, nonpartisan city races, the Authority's board elections with the Nation's seat, and the Nation's own elections. A political leaning by sub-region, tied to the decade, and the fights that really divide the region.
12. **The city of Neptune and Cadence.** Neighborhoods, the port, the shipyard district, the hospital, the ballpark and the airport.

Each finished piece goes into the Master Reference, with its own companion file where it needs one.
