# Workshop Notes

## The build queue

The order matters. The Singee language comes before the accent, because the accent borrows from it. The history comes before the university and the politics, because both stand on it. The two cities come last, because they draw on everything else.

1. **The repository and its house rules.** Done, November 2026.
2. **The road atlas.** Done, November 2026. *Road Atlas* v1.0, with the Master Reference updated to v3.0.
3. **Singee: sound and spelling.** Done, November 2026. *The Singee Language, Sound and Spelling* v1.0, with the Master Reference updated to v3.1. Four vowels and no O. Seventeen consonants, five of them edges the clerks smoothed away (the catch, popped t' and k', the breathy lh, the rasp x). Stress on the root. Clerk spelling against the Nation's 1988 keyboard spelling. Nurven is Nuxven in Singee and stays Nurven by the 1973 vote. Narration uses its viewpoint character's spelling. First new words: xalhi, fog; t'una, cedar; k'avi, rain.
4. **Singee: grammar.** Done, November 2026. *The Singee Language, Grammar* v1.0, with the Master Reference updated to v3.2. The verb comes first. Five evidence endings: seen, sensed, figured, told, and the old telling. Finished and not-yet instead of tense. Water directions: seaward, inland, across. No he or she, but here (sa) and not here (xe). Two kinds of we (kin and nan). Plurals by repeating a syllable. Close and loose ownership. New words: yen, suli, seni, k'ura, lhuni, ma, hu, u, and the people words. New words now pass `tools/singee_check.py`.
5. **Singee: the word list.** Done, November 2026. *The Singee Language, Word List* v1.0, with 225 entries across twelve sections, the Grammar updated to v1.1 (compounds, word order, repeated verbs) and the Master Reference updated to v3.3. Every word has both spellings and a say-it guide, generated from `tools/singee_words.tsv`. Counting is in fives and tens. Singee place names: Tumi, Yamu, Xu'a, Ikenu'i, Ruka'i, Vasa'isa (Five Fords is its translation), Lhemilhun, Lhunpasi, Vevexi, Yamuvexi, Hevu, Tumiyamu. Words with no English match: kenu, xaxalhi, hetaya, rukesu, lhun'a, yalhisa, xenula, sayen and xeyen, k'ek'eli, yelika, vekani, lhun. Clerk spelling collapses pulhi and puli, k'itu and kitu, and k'ura and k'uxa. A telling of the 1964 flood at Five Fords from a 1987 archive tape.
6. **Singee history.** Done, November 2026. *The Singee, A History* v1.0, with the Master Reference updated to v3.4. Covers the Nurven confederation's autumn gathering at Vasa'isa; the fever years of 1830 to 1834 (pulhi masi), which mostly spared the high country; the 1856 removals under other names; Lucius V. Thorne's 1894 cylinders and 1896 "corrupted dialect" monograph; Chemawa; the denied 1912 and 1931 petitions; termination under other names in 1954; the 1969 testimony in Singee; the 1970 linguist who proved Thorne wrong with his own cylinders; recognition in 1973; the cylinder copies of the 1980s and the 1988 alphabet; the Return desk in 1990; Thorne's notebooks home in 2009; the Nation's businesses; the 2012 preschool; and eleven first-language speakers in 2026. The Singee's own origin account stays with the Nation.
7. **The regional accent.** Done, November 2026. *Neptune English, How the Region Talks* v1.0, with the Master Reference updated to v3.5. Nothing is ever spelled phonetically. It's built on real Northwest English (the cot–caught merger, *bag* toward *vague*, OR-ih-gun, wil-AM-it). Place nicknames with "the." Local pronunciations that expose outsiders: Wrack is rack, Faust rhymes with lost, Nela is NEE-luh. Milepost and river directions, Low Water hours, tagless, sunbreak, the Sisters are out, check the board. Chinook Jargon and logging layers, and the Singee borrowings lun, kenuing, the rally and kura. Variation by place and by decade, writing rules, and two voice samples.
8. **The missing middle of the century.** Done, November 2026. *The Greater Neptune Region, The Twentieth Century* v1.0, with the Master Reference updated to v3.6 (timeline, settlements, the Authority and the settled appendix) and *Neptune English* updated to v1.1 for the Rivers. Splash dams, then the Bay line in 1916 and its 1914 swing bridge. The Strand Timber Company's railroad up Lake Creek, Camps One to Eight, end of track at Camp Nine in 1926, rails up in 1948. Spruce soldiers north of Wrack in 1918. The Cascade line to East Wild in 1912 and over the pass in 1926. North Fork Lumber's company town at Nela Junction, 1923 to 1984, and Marlow Pacific at East Wild, 1948 to 1987. The Bay Yard, the Neptune Bay Shipbuilding Corporation, 1942 to 1946, with 141 Liberty and 38 Victory ships and hiring halls in Shreveport, Houston, Beaumont and Pine Bluff. Harbor Homes on Cannery Slough, the Rivers and its river-named streets, Galilee Missionary Baptist Church (1942, Reverend Emmett Batiste) and the auxiliary firings of 1943. The Harbor Center renewal of 1963 to 1966 cleared the Rivers' western half for the civic district, the Authority bought the land in 1978, and the city hasn't paid. Coast Guard beach patrol at Ichabod's Eye, 1943 to 1944. The timber wars on the real federal record, with the Fawn Creek blockade on the pattern of Warner Creek, and the argument about awake and asleep kept as a public belief with no new deep-register rule. Every real date and figure checked against sources, and every Mirth date checked against the Tithe's archive and encyclopedia entry.
9. **Legends and atmosphere.** Done, November 2026. *The Greater Neptune Region, Legends and the Forest's Year* v1.0, with the Master Reference updated to v3.7 (Ichabod's Eye, the fixtures and the settled appendix) and the *Road Atlas* updated to v1.1 so the Lake always means Lake Varuna. Twenty-six legends, each marked told everywhere (truth open, for the book set there to decide), true nowhere (false, with the explanation) or true somewhere and saved for the book set there. Ichabod's Eye's ghost is the Patrol: Seaman Second Class Eldon Pike, lost in the surf in February 1944 with the beach patrol, whose dog Bosun came back alone. He walks north toward Wrack on fog nights with an empty leash, and the Eye's count runs one long. No legend touches a held question, and Mirth's and the Singee's stories stay with their owners. The forest's year, season by season, on real Oregon ecology, plus the smells that date a scene: wigwam burners, canneries, grass-seed smoke, the smoke months.
10. **The University of Greater Neptune.** Done, November 2026. *The University of Greater Neptune* v1.0, with the Master Reference updated to v3.8 (a new Institutions section, the city, Cadence and the Wilds, the timeline and the settled appendix). The city campus began in 1946 as a college for veterans in the Bay Yard's empty buildings, on the Vanport pattern, and stands on the slipways (the Ways) and the mold loft (the Loft). UGNC in Cadence opened in 1966 and has forestry and fire, a late start for fire crews that the chemistry department ignores, and the pool where the clubs train before dawn. The Normal has been in East Wild since 1931. The Gate campus opened in 1981 for working adults and the Authority's staff. Varuna Station opened in 1958 and keeps old-growth reference stands on the Andrews pattern. Lhunpasi College opened at the Fold in 1985 and stays outside the system by the council's one-sentence answer. The legislature created the system in 1979, and it has had its own board since 2015, with a trustee nominated by the Nation. Pietro's UGNC and Fiver's pool match the Tithe's archive. The legends file is now v1.1 and the Twentieth Century v1.1, so that the slough's canneries closed in the 1960s while the port's fish plant runs on, as *Low Water* has it. *Neptune English* v1.2 adds the Normal.
11. **Politics.** Done, November 2026. *The Greater Neptune Region, Politics* v1.0, with the Master Reference updated to v3.9 (the Authority, the Nation today, the timeline and the settled appendix) and *Neptune English* updated to v1.3. Oregon votes by mail, and city, district, sheriff, judicial and Authority races carry no party label. Eight real counties sit under the line and every courthouse is outside it. Lane holds most of the region, Linn's commission is partisan, Cadence votes in Jefferson and Deschutes at once, and Faust sits on the Klamath line. The Authority's board is the Nine: the Governor's Eight from 1977, then the May 1986 vote that Erase the Line campaigned for as abolition and that created an elected board instead, on the pattern of Portland's 1978 Metro vote. Seven zones (Harbor, Bay, Waist, Santiam, McKenzie, Crest, South), a chair elected region-wide (Hollis Marr first, in 1987) and the Nation's seat, whose member steps out of every overlay vote. The Nation's 1975 constitution: a council of seven, a count at the Fold every September read out in Singee since 2024, the four-seat residency rule, the December check and the 2011 enrollment window. Each sub-region's leaning is dated by decade, and no town keeps a permanent party. The fights: the forest's money and county payments, the line on the map, the Authority itself, water at the Sump and Sweetbriar, fire and the hazard map, second homes, the Nation's consent, the Rivers and Greater Idaho. The Sump now burns the same night as the Rainbelt in 2020. Present-day officeholders stay unnamed for each book to fill.
12. **The city of Neptune and Cadence.** Done, November 2026. *The Greater Neptune Region, Neptune and Cadence* v1.0, with the Master Reference updated to v3.10 and *Politics* to v1.1 for the finished ward cross-reference. Neptune's six wards, working port commission, Choke pilots, active terminal and fish plant, surviving ship-repair trades beside UGN's Ways, the Rivers and Harbor Center, Neptune Bay Medical Center, Railbank Field and the Pilots, and the fogbound commuter airport. Cadence's eight neighborhoods, Jefferson–Deschutes seam, UGNC pool, Cadence Basin Medical Center, Switchback Field and the Switchbacks, aquifer and fire politics, and Redmond Airport access outside the line. All intercity timings remain with the Road Atlas; no new road or Singee word was needed. The Tithe's Mirth and Pietro files were checked for alignment; no changes were required there.

Each finished piece goes into the Master Reference, with its own companion file where it needs one.
