# The Neptune Continuum

The shared world of every story set in the Greater Neptune Region: a 6,060-square-mile bioregion wrapped around Neptune National Forest, laid over real Oregon between the central coast and the high desert.

The stories are separate. They share a forest, a map, a road, a few winters everyone remembers, and one old woman nobody quite notices.

## What's here

- `canon/Greater Neptune Region - Master Reference v3.md` is the authority for the region.
- `canon/Greater Neptune Region - Road Atlas.md` holds every milepost, distance and drive time, plus airports, rail, forest roads and what closes in winter.
- `canon/The Singee Language - Sound and Spelling.md` sets out how Singee sounds and its two spellings, the Authority's and the Nation's.
- `canon/The Singee Language - Grammar.md` covers how Singee sentences are built, from evidence endings to water directions.
- `canon/The Singee Language - Word List.md` holds the Singee vocabulary in both spellings, with a guide to saying each word.
- `canon/The Singee - A History.md` is the public history of the Singee, from the confederation to the language revival.
- `canon/Neptune English - How the Region Talks.md` covers the region's accent, words and speech habits, and how to write them without phonetic spelling.
- `canon/Greater Neptune Region - The Twentieth Century.md` is the region's public history from 1900 to 2000: the rail camps, the Bay Yard and the Rivers, the long timber boom and the timber wars.
- `canon/Greater Neptune Region - The University of Greater Neptune.md` covers the region's public university, its four campuses and field station, and the Nation's own college at the Fold.
- `canon/Greater Neptune Region - Legends and the Forest's Year.md` holds the legend list, each legend marked told everywhere, true nowhere or true somewhere and saved, the Patrol at Ichabod's Eye, and how the forest smells and sounds season by season.
- `canon/Mrs. Birdsong - Character File v2.md` covers the one character who crosses between stories.
- `canon/Low Water - Program Bible.md` covers the late-night radio show everyone in the region has heard.
- `tools/road_model.py` is the road model behind the atlas's numbers, `tools/singee_check.py` checks new Singee words against the sound rules, and `tools/build_word_list.py` builds the word list from `tools/singee_words.tsv`.
- `Workshop Notes.md` is the build queue.
- `CLAUDE.md` holds the working rules for Claude.

Every canon document comes in Markdown and Word.
